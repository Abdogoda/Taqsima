# ⚽ Taqseema

> **Build fair football teams. Manage every session. Track every player.**

Taqseema is a modern football session management platform designed for the way we actually play football in Egypt and similar communities.

Instead of being just another pitch booking app, Taqseema helps organizers manage everything that happens **before, during, and after** a football session.

---

# ✨ Features

## 👤 Player Management

* Create and manage your player database.
* Add player photo.
* Assign preferred position.
* Set a manual **Overall Rating (0–100)**.
* Mark players as injured or unavailable.
* Keep all your regular players in one place.

---

## ⚽ Football Sessions

Create football sessions with:

* 📅 Date
* ⏰ Start & End Time
* ⌛ Duration
* 📍 Field Name & Location
* 👥 Players per Team (5v5, 6v6, 7v7...)
* 🏆 Number of Teams

Each organizer can create unlimited sessions.

---

## 🤝 Smart Team Builder

Create teams in multiple ways:

* ✋ Manual team selection
* 🤖 Automatic balanced teams based on player ratings
* 📋 Reuse previously saved team templates

Need to change players later?

No problem.

* Move players between teams.
* Rename teams.
* Change team colors.
* Create new teams.
* Remove existing teams.

Everything is flexible.

---

## 📦 Team Templates

Save your favorite teams and reuse them anytime.

Perfect for regular football groups that play together every week.

Templates can be:

* Reused
* Updated
* Deleted
* Duplicated

Each football session works on its own copy, so previous sessions are never affected.

---

## 🔄 Flexible Match Rotation

Support different play styles:

* Two fixed teams
* Winner stays
* Loser stays
* Timed rotation
* Manual rotation

The organizer decides how the session will run.

---

## 📊 Match Statistics

Record:

* ⚽ Goals
* 🎯 Assists
* 🏅 Man of the Match
* 🥇 Winners
* 🥈 Losers

Player statistics are updated automatically after each session.

---

## 📈 Player Statistics

Each player has a football profile including:

* Matches Played
* Wins
* Losses
* Goals
* Assists
* Own Goals
* Clean Sheets
* Man of the Match Awards
* Attendance
* Minutes Played

The **Overall Rating** is always managed manually by the organizer.
